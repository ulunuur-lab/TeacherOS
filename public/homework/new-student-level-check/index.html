Yangi O'quvchi Uchun Katta Diagnostik Level Check

Ushbu test faqat o'tilgan 2 ta darsni (Present Simple va Present Continuous) chuqur va har tomonlama tekshiradi. Hech qanday o'rganilmagan mavzular yo'q. Har bir savolga javob berganda qoidani bilib olasiz!

1️⃣ Present Simple (Odatlar & Faktlar)
I/You/We/They → play / don't play
He/She/It → plays / doesn't play
Kalit so'zlar: every day, always, usually, often, never
2️⃣ Present Continuous (Hozir sodir bo'layotgan ish)
am / is / are + V-ing
I am playing / He is playing / They are playing
Kalit so'zlar: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
3️⃣ Stative Verbs (Holat fe'llari)
Hech qachon -ing olmaydi: like, love, want, need, know
"I want coffee now" ✅ (I am wanting emas ❌)

☀️ 1-Bo'lim: Present Simple — Odatlar va Qoidalar (10 ta Savol)
Egalarga qarab to'g'ri fe'l shaklini tanlang (V1 yoki V-s/es, don't yoki doesn't, Do yoki Does).
⚡ 2-Bo'lim: Present Continuous — Ayni Damdagi Harakatlar (10 ta Savol)
Hozir sodir bo'layotgan harakatlar uchun to'g'ri am / is / are + V-ing shaklini tanlang.
🧠 3-Bo'lim: Holat Fe'llari (Stative Verbs Qopqoni — 8 ta Savol)
Eslatma: like, love, want, need, know, understand kabi fe'llar hissiyot va fikrni bildiradi. Ular hatto "now" kelsa ham -ing olmaydi!
⚖️ 4-Bo'lim: Present Simple vs Present Continuous Qiyosi (10 ta Savol)
Kalit so'zlarga e'tibor bering: every day, usually → Simple; now, at the moment, Look! → Continuous.
🔍 5-Bo'lim: Mistake Hunter — Xatoni Qanday Tuzatamiz? (8 ta Qopqon)
Har bir gapdagi xatoni toping va to'g'ri variantni tanlang.
🧩 6-Bo'lim: Gap Tuzuvchi (Sentence Builder — 4 ta Mashq)
So'zlarni to'g'ri tartibda bosib mukammal gap hosil qiling!
✍️ 7-Bo'lim: Oddiy Tarjima (5 ta Gap)
Berilgan yordamchi so'zlar bilan gaplarni ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.
