A1 Elementary Present Perfect Mastery Learning By Doing

Present Perfect: A1 O'rganish & Mustahkamlash Laboratoriyasi

Present Perfect zamonini tushunish juda oson! Ushbu mashg'ulotda har bir savol orqali qoidalarni bosqichma-bosqich o'rganib borasiz. Hech qanday qiyin so'zlar yo'q — faqat kundalik hayotdagi oddiy ingliz tili.

1️⃣ HAVE yoki HAS?
I / You / We / They → HAVE
He / She / It / Aziz → HAS
"I have visited" / "She has visited"
2️⃣ V3 (O'tgan Sifatdosh)
To'g'ri fe'l: play → played, live → lived
Noto'g'ri fe'l: see → seen, eat → eaten
"have played" / "have eaten"
3️⃣ FOR yoki SINCE?
FOR = Qancha vaqt? (for 2 days, for 3 years)
SINCE = Qachondan boshlab? (since 2021, since Monday)
"for 2 hours" / "since 9 o'clock"
0 / 55
Javob berildi: 0 / 55

🔊 1-Qism: Kundalik A1 Fe'llar va Vaqt Iboralari (Audio)
Tugmachani bosib so'zlarning to'g'ri inglizcha talaffuzini eshiting va tarjimasi bilan tanishing.
⚡ 2-Qism: HAVE yoki HAS? (10 ta Savol)
Qoida: I, You, We, They → HAVE. He, She, It va bitta odam (Aziz, My sister) → HAS.
🎯 3-Qism: To'g'ri V3 Fe'l Shaklini Tanlang (10 ta Savol)
Present Perfect zamonida fe'l har doim 3-shaklda (V3) bo'ladi. Har bir savolda to'g'ri V3 shaklini toping.
⏱️ 4-Qism: FOR yoki SINCE? (15 ta Kundalik Savol)
Qoida: Agar vaqt soni bo'lsa (2 soat, 3 kun, 5 yil) → FOR oling.
Agar boshlangan vaqt nomi bo'lsa (kecha, dushanba, soat 9, 2020-yil) → SINCE oling.
🔍 5-Qism: Mistake Hunter — Xatoni Qanday Tuzatamiz? (10 ta Qopqon)
Har bir gapda 1 ta xato bor. Variantlardan to'g'ri tuzatishni bosing va qoidani bilib oling!
🧩 6-Qism: Gap Tuzuvchi (Sentence Builder — 5 ta Mashq)
So'zlarni to'g'ri tartibda bosib to'liq Present Perfect gapini hosil qiling!
💡 7-Qism: Kundalik Vaziyatlar Testi (5 ta Savol)
Haqiqiy kundalik suhbatlarga mos to'g'ri javobni tanlang.
✍️ 8-Qism: Oddiy Tarjima (6 ta Gap)
Yordamchi so'zlar (hint) yordamida gaplarni ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.
🌟 9-Qism: O'zingiz Haqida 3 ta Kichik Gap
Berilgan namuna asosida o'zingiz haqida 1 tadan qisqa va chiroyli gap yozing:
1. Shahringizda qancha vaqtdan beri yashaysiz?
Namuna: I have lived in Tashkent for 10 years. yoki I have lived in Samarkand since 2015.
2. Qancha vaqtdan beri ingliz tili o'rganasiz?
Namuna: I have studied English for 3 months. yoki I have studied English since September.
3. O'zbekistonning qaysi go'zal joyini ko'rgansiz?
Namuna: I have visited Bukhara. yoki I have seen Chimgan mountains.
