1️⃣ Past Tenses Hierarchy
Past Simple: finished action (V2)
Past Continuous: was/were + V-ing
Past Perfect: had + V3 (earlier past)
2️⃣ Future Time Clauses & Forms
when / as soon as / until + Present Simple
Future Continuous: will be doing
Future Perfect: will have done (by the time)
3️⃣ Future in the Past & Backshift
Niyat: was/were going to + V1
Va'da / Kelajak: would + V1
Reported: will → would, am → was

🕰️ 1-Bo'lim: Past Simple, Continuous & Past Perfect (10 ta Savol)
O'tmishdagi voqealar ketma-ketligi, davom etgan jarayonlar va boshqa o'tgan ishdan oldin sodir bo'lgan harakatlar (had + V3).
🚀 2-Bo'lim: Kelajak Zamonlar va Vaqt Bog'lovchilari (When, As soon as, Until — 10 ta Savol)
When, as soon as, before, after, until dan keyin kelajak uchun HOZIRGI zamon (Present Simple) qo'llanilishi va 4 xil kelajak shakli qiyosi.
⚡ 3-Bo'lim: Future Continuous & Future Perfect (10 ta Savol)
Aniq kelajak vaqtda davom etayotgan harakat (will be doing) hamda kelajak muddatgacha tugallanadigan ish (will have done).
🔮 4-Bo'lim: Future in the Past (Was/Were going to vs Would — 8 ta Savol)
O'tmishdagi amalga oshmagan reja va niyatlar (was going to) hamda o'tmishda turib kelajak haqida berilgan va'dalar (would).
🗣️ 5-Bo'lim: O'zlashtirma Gapda Zamonlarning Orqaga Surilishi (Tense Backshifts — 5 ta Savol)
To'g'ri gapni o'zlashtirma gapga aylantirganda zamonlarning bir pog'ona orqaga o'zgarishi: will → would, past simple → past perfect.
🔍 6-Bo'lim: B1 Mistake Hunter (8 ta Katta Qopqon)
Zamonlar uyg'unligi va nozik grammatik chalkashliklarni toping va to'g'irlang.
✍️ 7-Bo'lim: Kontekstual B1 Tarjima (5 ta Gap)
O'zbekcha gaplarni B1 zamon qoidalariga rioya qilgan holda to'liq ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.
